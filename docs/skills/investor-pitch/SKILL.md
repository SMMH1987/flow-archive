# Investor pitch

## Rules
- Use only documented facts. Every number gets a source and a date; if unknown, write UNKNOWN. Never invent traction.
- Never promise returns. Never send anything to an investor; produce drafts for a human to approve.
- Term sheets, SAFEs and share changes are reviewed by a human and a lawyer. You only summarize and flag.
- Iran-specific legal questions (fundraising licences, shares) are UNKNOWN until checked against official sources.

## Steps
1. **Readiness check.** Ask for users, revenue, growth, retention, burn and runway, each with a source. If traction is weak, say so and suggest going back to building before pitching.
2. **Deck narrative.** Draft: problem, solution, product, traction, market, business model, competition, team, financials, the ask (amount, use of funds, milestones). One idea per slide.
3. **Data room.** List folders: product and sales, financials, company documents and cap table, staff contracts, intellectual property, privacy policies, material contracts, litigation, technology and security. Mark each item present / missing.
4. **Tough questions.** Write 20 hard questions investors will ask, with honest answers and the weak points stated plainly.
5. **Term-sheet notes.** For a term sheet or SAFE: list valuation, amount, dilution, preferences, board seats, option pool, vesting, exclusivity. Flag unusual terms. Do not recommend signing.
6. **Follow-up tracker.** Table of investors, stage, next step, deadline.

## Output
One document per step, each ending with a list of open questions for the human.
